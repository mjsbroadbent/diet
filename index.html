How these numbers are worked out
  • Resting burn (BMR) uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age, then +5 for men or −161 for women.
  • Maintenance (TDEE) is BMR × your activity multiplier.
  • The goal adds or removes calories per day. 7,700 kcal is roughly 1 kg of body fat.
  • Protein is set per kg of body weight (more when cutting to keep muscle). With a BMI over 30 it uses the weight at BMI 25 instead, so the target stays realistic.
  • Fat is 30% of calories (25% if carbs would drop too low). Carbs fill the rest. Keto fixes net carbs at 30 g and fat fills the rest.
  • Calories never go below 1,500 for men or 1,200 for women without medical supervision.
  • These are estimates, typically within ±10%. Weigh in for 2 to 3 weeks and adjust by 100 to 200 kcal if the trend is off.
